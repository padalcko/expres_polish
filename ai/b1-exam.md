# State Polish Language Exam B1

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Definition and audience

The state certification examination assesses practical Polish language proficiency at B1. A successful candidate can obtain an official certificate of language proficiency. Whether it is suitable for a particular administrative, educational or professional purpose depends on that procedure's requirements.

This document summarizes the existing Expres Polish B1 pages; it does not add new examination thresholds, dates, fees or eligibility rules.

## Skills and structure

- Listening: understand spoken Polish and identify relevant information.
- Reading: understand main ideas and specific information in Polish texts.
- Grammar: use grammatical structures and vocabulary correctly.
- Writing: complete written tasks for a given situation.
- Speaking: express ideas independently and communicate in Polish.

## Preparation at Expres Polish

Preparation includes assessing current skills, learning the examination format and practising regularly. Learners work on listening, reading, grammar, writing and speaking. In-person preparation is available in Wrocław, as is online preparation. The preparation period depends on starting level, regularity and independent practice.

## FAQ

### Can I prepare online?

Yes. Expres Polish offers online preparation as well as classes in Wrocław.

### Where is the examination taken?

State examinations are organized by authorized examination centres. Check which centres participate in the relevant session before registering.

### How long does preparation take?

There is no universal duration. It depends on current proficiency and the time available for classes and independent study.

### How should I start?

Identify your current level and the skills needing most work, then contact Expres Polish about a preparation format.
