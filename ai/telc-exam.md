# TELC Polish Language Exam

Last updated and fact-checked: 2026-10-07

## Definition and levels

TELC (The European Language Certificates) is a language examination system. The official Polish catalogue lists telc Język polski B1·B2 Szkoła, a dual-level examination assessing CEFR B1 and B2. Its themes are designed around school students' interests. Adult candidates should confirm participation conditions and the exact examination with their chosen centre.

## Exam structure and skills

The examination has written and oral components:

- Listening: 4 parts, 25 minutes.
- Reading: 4 parts, 40 minutes.
- Language elements: 2 parts, 20 minutes.
- Writing: 1 part, 30 minutes.
- Speaking: 3 parts, approximately 16 minutes, usually with 2 candidates; 20 minutes of preparation.

The written component totals 115 minutes without a break. The official page lists paper-based and hybrid delivery; it cannot be taken as a partial examination. Online preparation does not imply an at-home examination.

Tasks include selecting answers, matching information, a written response, image description, a presentation and discussion. Practice materials illustrate task formats, not the topics of a future examination session.

## Assessment

Three result blocks determine the outcome: listening/reading/language elements together, writing, and speaking. B1 certification requires at least B1 in speaking and at least one of the other two blocks. B2 certification requires B2 in speaking and at least one of the other two blocks. Speaking below B1 does not qualify for a certificate.

Writing criteria cover task fulfilment, organization, accuracy and vocabulary. Speaking also considers pronunciation/intonation and fluency. See printed pages 33–39 of the official mock examination for scoring details; there is no single overall percentage rule for all components.

## TELC vs State B1

These are different certification systems. The state B1 preparation page addresses a specific state examination level; Polish TELC B1·B2 assesses two levels within one examination. Task structures and assessment rules differ. Choose according to the receiving institution's requirements, not a claim that either examination is universally better.

## Recognition and limitations

Polish official guidance includes TELC certificates at B1 or above among accepted language evidence for the EU long-term resident permit in Poland. This addresses the language requirement, not all conditions for a permit.

Recognition as a Polish citizen follows different documentary requirements: TELC does not replace the state certificate specified for that procedure. For education or employment, verify the exact certificate and level required by the receiving institution. No claim of universal acceptance is made.

## Registration

Register with a TELC examination centre offering the Polish examination. Confirm the examination name, date, price, registration deadline, identification documents, cancellation terms and any accessibility arrangements. Centres do not necessarily offer every language.

Expres Polish offers preparation; the website does not claim official TELC examination-centre status. A school preparation enquiry is not examination registration.

## FAQ

### Is there a speaking component?

Yes. It has three parts, normally with two participants, and preparation time.

### Is there a written component?

Yes. It includes listening, reading, language elements and writing.

### Can I prepare online or in Wrocław?

Yes, Expres Polish offers both preparation settings.

### How long should I prepare?

There is no fixed duration for everyone. Current skills, target level and study time determine the plan.

### Where do I register and what does it cost?

Contact the examination centre. It confirms session availability, deadlines and its examination fee; preparation is arranged separately with the school.
